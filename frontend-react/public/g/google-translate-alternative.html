Why this is different

For a quick read of general text Google Translate is enough, and we do not claim to beat it on a single sentence. Where the two products part is after the sentence: the file, the glossary, and who is accountable for the output.

Cost is published, not hidden: the estimator on our compare page uses the same formula as billing, and you see the estimate before you commit.

Questions

Why not paste the text into a free translator?

Because the paste is the small part. Rebuilding a slide deck, a spec sheet or a contract after translation is the labor nobody puts in the price.

Does terminology carry across documents?

Yes. A knowledge package plus translation memory means a term decided in March is still the same term in September, on every file your team uploads.

Can it be called from our own systems?

There is an OpenAPI and SDK for that, with the same points accounting as the interface — so usage stays in one budget instead of a second one.

How do we try it on a real file?

Upload one document you would actually ship. Compare the download against the upload; layout and terminology are visible in the file itself.
